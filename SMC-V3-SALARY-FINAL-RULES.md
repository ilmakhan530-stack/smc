SMC V3 — Salary Final Rules
1. Staff official start: 09:00 AM. Earlier arrival does not create extra paid time.
2. Staff: no overtime, no Sunday 2x.
3. Labour: monthly salary / calendar days.
4. Labour Sunday: the worked day is paid 2x.
5. Labour Sunday extra hours beyond 8 are OT at 2x OT rate.
6. Monday-Saturday labour OT is automatic from In/Out beyond 8 hours.
7. Salary sheet shows OT HOURS, not OT pay.
8. Advance is entered only in the separate Advance module and automatically deducted from salary balance.
9. Salary History shows Advance History with date and amount.
10. Salary History shows Deduction amount and Paid/Unpaid status.
11. Salary History shows Payment Notes.
12. Positive AND negative final balances carry forward to the next month.
13. A positive carry-forward means previous month credit; a negative carry-forward means previous month due/amount owed.
14. Clicking a person's name opens complete salary history/details.
