SMC V3 — Salary Final Rules
1. Staff official start: 09:00 AM. Earlier arrival does not create extra paid time.
2. Staff: no overtime, no Sunday 2x.
3. Labour: monthly salary / calendar days.
4. Labour Sunday: the worked day is paid 2x (normal day + equal Sunday premium).
5. Labour Sunday extra hours beyond 8 are OT at 2x OT rate.
6. Labour Monday-Saturday OT is automatic from In/Out beyond 8 hours.
7. Salary sheet shows OT HOURS, not OT pay.
8. Salary sheet does not show an Advance column; advances are entered in the separate Advance module and are automatically deducted.
9. Deduction input and Paid/Unpaid status are available in Salary.
10. Negative balance carries forward to next month.
11. Clicking a person's name opens salary history/details.
12. Salary History now shows an Advance History table with every advance date and amount for the selected month, plus the total advance.
