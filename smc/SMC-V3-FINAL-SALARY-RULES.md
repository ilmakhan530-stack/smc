# SMC V3 FINAL SALARY RULES

STAFF
- Official start time is 09:00 AM.
- Coming before 09:00 AM does not create extra working time.
- Staff has NO overtime.
- Staff receives NO Sunday 2x benefit.
- Staff Sunday work does not create OT.

LABOUR
- Monthly salary is divided by the number of calendar days in that month.
- Present = 1 day; Half Day = 0.5 day; Absent = 0.
- Labour Sunday work counts as a Present day.
- Full 8-hour Sunday work gets 2x payment: normal Sunday day pay plus an equal Sunday premium.
- Sunday hours beyond the normal 8 hours are actual OT only.
- Sunday OT is paid at 2x the normal OT rate.
- Monday-Saturday OT is based on actual time beyond 8 hours.
- OT is calculated automatically from In Time and Out Time.
- Show OT HOURS, never Yes/No.
- Sunday premium and OT money are included in the main balance, not separate salary-sheet payment columns.
- Advance is recorded only in the separate Advance module.
- Deduction remains in Salary.
- Paid/Unpaid remains in Salary.
- Negative balance carries forward to the next month.
