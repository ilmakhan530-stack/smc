# Salary History + Carry Forward

Salary employee name should open a detail modal/page with:
- monthly salary
- previous/carry-forward balance
- present, absent, half day
- daily date, in time, out time
- overtime hours (not Yes)
- advance dates and amounts
- earned salary, Sunday premium and overtime included in balance
- final balance

Carry-forward:
previous month's final balance becomes current month's opening balance.
Negative balances continue into future months until cleared.
