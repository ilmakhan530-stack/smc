# Thekedar Monthly Carry Forward Fix

- Balance is calculated per contractor and per selected month.
- Opening Carry Forward = all work minus advances minus payments from earlier months.
- Closing Balance = Opening Carry Forward + current-month work - current-month advances - current-month payments.
- Positive closing balance is shown as Unpaid / payable.
- Negative closing balance is shown as Carry Forward / credit and automatically becomes part of the next month's opening balance.
- Zero closing balance is shown as Paid.
- Payment notes remain visible in Payment History.
- Thekedar records remain separate from Labour/Staff salary and attendance.
